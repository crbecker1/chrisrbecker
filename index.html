About me

Design is a making praxis. What you make at first will likely not be very good and that means you are learning to make it better. Just Keep Making!

I’m a senior UX/product designer, and design educatorwho has spent my career moving between shipping products and teaching the people who will design the next ones. That loop keeps my work honest: what I teach has to survive contact with real teams, and what I build has to be explainable to a first-year student.

I believe in small, evidence-led steps, clear critique, and craft that serves people before it serves a portfolio. Whether I’m leading a discovery sprint or mentoring a career-changer, the goal is the same — better questions, clearer decisions, and work you’re proud to explain.
