The quick version

The Deutsch Inc. team needed to be mixed up and challenged to ideate with some new strategies. As a consultant for Open Door Consulting, I designed and ran two 90-minute remote ideation sessions: What if / We like / We wish to loosen up a stiff group, then Five Hats to stretch their concepts and get them thinking broadly.

The sessions produced a lively set of ideas from a team that had been struggling to ideate in a meaningful way.
