Personal project

CRB|40: Shaping My Own Surfboard

For my 40th birthday, my wife gave me the chance to shape my own surfboard. I used my graphic design background to make it mine.

The quick version

In 2021 I turned 40. My wonderful wife got me a present I would never have thought to ask for: shaping my own surfboard with the suppliers at FoamEZ.

I ran it like any other design project. I collected inspiration, sketched in a notebook, built concepts in Figma, and then took the design from the screen to a block of foam. My graphic design background is what made the board personal: the shapes, the colors, and the CRB|40 mark are all mine.

Outcome

  • It gets noticed. I get a lot of “rad” comments on the board, from El Porto to County Line.
  • It spread. I have recommended surfboard shaping to multiple friends.
  • It lasts. Five years after I made it, the board still keeps me happy and satisfied.
