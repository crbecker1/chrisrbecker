## About Chris

Chris is a senior UX designer and educator based in Los Angeles. He teaches at Otis College of Art and Design, and has teaching affiliations in UX/UI at ArtCenter College of Design and interaction design at Santa Monica College. He also writes about design and technology.

## About this site

This is the source repository for Chris's personal website. The site brings his professional work, writing, and teaching together in one place.
